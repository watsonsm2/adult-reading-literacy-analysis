# Research Methodology

## Research Design

This study will use a quantitative, observational research design to investigate factors associated with reading fluency and comprehension among adult readers.

The research will prioritize secondary analysis of existing datasets and will incorporate descriptive statistics, exploratory data analysis, multivariable regression, and predictive machine learning.

## Target Population

The study population will consist of adults aged 18 years and older.

Eligible datasets may include university students, working adults, adult learners, and other adult reading populations.

## Data Collection

Potential sources include:

- OneStop Eye Movements Dataset
- Programme for the International Assessment of Adult Competencies (PIAAC)
- Ghent Eye-Tracking Corpus (GECO)
- CELER Eye-Tracking Corpus

The final dataset will be selected based on population relevance, availability, documented reading assessments, predictor variables, licensing, and research validity.

## Variables

### Dependent Variables

1. Reading fluency or a validated reading-efficiency measure.
2. Reading comprehension performance.

### Independent Variables

Potential predictors include vocabulary knowledge, decoding ability, cognitive performance, reading habits, educational background, reading motivation, language experience, and passage difficulty.

Only variables supported by the selected dataset will be included in the final analysis.

## Data Analysis

The analytical process will include:

1. Dataset acquisition and documentation.
2. Data quality evaluation and preprocessing.
3. Exploratory data analysis.
4. Correlation and multivariable regression analysis.
5. Feature engineering and selection.
6. Machine learning model development.
7. Model evaluation and interpretation.
8. Research reporting and visualization.

Potential machine learning methods include regularized regression, Random Forest, and gradient boosting.

Continuous prediction targets may be evaluated using MAE, RMSE, and R².

The project will assess model performance on held-out data and use appropriate procedures to prevent data leakage.

## Ethical Considerations

The research will prioritize participant confidentiality, permitted data use, valid measurement, transparent reporting, and appropriate interpretation.

Associations and machine learning feature importance will not be interpreted as evidence of causation.

## Limitations

Potential limitations include differences in assessment instruments, sampling methods, language backgrounds, text difficulty, missing data, and generalizability.

Eye-tracking measurements will not automatically be treated as direct measures of reading fluency without supporting validation evidence.

## Expected Deliverables

- Cleaned and documented analysis datasets or reproducible data-access instructions.
- Statistical analyses and visualizations.
- Evaluated machine learning models.
- Model interpretation and limitations report.
- Final research findings and recommendations.
