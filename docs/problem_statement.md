# Research Problem Statement

## Background

Reading fluency and reading comprehension are essential components of literacy that influence how individuals access, process, interpret, and apply written information.

For adult readers, these abilities play important roles in education, employment, everyday decision-making, and lifelong learning.

Although reading fluency and comprehension are related, they represent distinct aspects of reading proficiency. Individuals may demonstrate efficient reading skills while experiencing difficulty understanding complex written material.

Differences in adult reading performance may be associated with linguistic, cognitive, behavioral, educational, and contextual factors, including vocabulary knowledge, working memory, reading habits, educational background, and text complexity.

## Problem Statement

Adult readers demonstrate varying levels of reading fluency and reading comprehension. However, the relative associations between these reading outcomes and individual linguistic, cognitive, behavioral, educational, and contextual characteristics require systematic investigation.

This research project aims to identify and evaluate the factors most strongly associated with differences in reading fluency and comprehension among adult readers.

Using statistical analysis and machine learning, the study will investigate relationships between reading performance, individual characteristics, reading behaviors, and educational experiences.

The research seeks to determine which measurable factors are most informative in explaining and predicting variation in adult reading performance.

## Research Purpose

The purpose of this study is to investigate factors associated with reading fluency and comprehension among adult readers through quantitative statistical analysis and machine learning.

The project aims to:

1. Identify measurable factors associated with adult reading fluency.
2. Identify factors associated with adult reading comprehension.
3. Examine the relationship between fluency and comprehension.
4. Compare the relative contributions of linguistic, cognitive, behavioral, and educational variables.
5. Develop and evaluate predictive models of adult reading performance.
6. Explore the implications of the findings for adult literacy research and educational support.

## Target Population

The target population consists of adult readers aged 18 years and older.

Depending on dataset availability, this may include university students, working adults, adult learners, and individuals with diverse educational backgrounds.

The actual study population and the extent to which findings can be generalized will be determined by the selected dataset.

## Research Significance

This project aims to contribute to a better understanding of adult literacy by identifying patterns in reading performance.

The findings may help inform future research related to literacy development, educational technology, personalized learning, and adult education.

The study will distinguish statistical association from causation and acknowledge limitations involving measurement, sampling, and generalizability.

## Expected Outcomes

The anticipated deliverables include:

- A reproducible statistical analysis of adult reading performance.
- An evaluation of factors associated with fluency and comprehension.
- Predictive machine learning models.
- Research visualizations and model interpretations.
- A final research report discussing findings, limitations, and educational implications.

These are planned deliverables rather than established research results.
